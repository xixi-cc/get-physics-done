# limiting-cases

Select limits justified by parameters, validity regimes and independent known
behavior. Identify the known-result source and match conventions. Show the
analytical limit or controlled expansion and exact difference; for numerical
checks approach the limit systematically and report error/ratio trends, not a
single point. Test noncommuting limit orders and boundary layers when relevant;
use test functions or moments for distributional limits. Classify exact match,
leading-order match, numerical agreement, discrepancy, divergence or cannot
check. Localize the earliest failed intermediate expression before debugging.
Do not require an approximation outside its stated validity domain to match.
