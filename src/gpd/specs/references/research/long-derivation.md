# Long derivations

Use for sustained analytical work or recovery of an unfinished derivation.
Keep ordinary reasoning in the main capable context. A long duration alone
is not a reason to delegate. Use fresh work for actual isolation, separable
parallel computation, or required independent proof review.

When the route is uncertain, plan the target, hypotheses, decisive obstacles
and acceptance evidence. Develop the method while working; do not invent a
known answer or fix every intermediate step in advance. Within the authorized
objective, revise the method when evidence warrants it. Changes to a locked
assumption, scientific contract or scope still use the corresponding gate.

At a useful mathematical boundary, preserve enough to resume: source/equation
locations, assumptions and domains, key intermediate expressions, approximation
orders and neglected terms, unresolved obligations, and rejected routes with
the actual reason they failed. Reuse the current derivation artifact and handoff;
state files index it. Do not create another parallel ledger or serialize private
reasoning. Save checkable mathematical work, evidence and decisions.

Save before context transfer and after a result costly to reconstruct, not
after every algebraic step. Keep full derivation records; no session-count or
line-count pruning. Resume from the latest relevant handoff and its cited
sources, expanding older material when dependencies require it. Missing evidence
remains missing; absence from a summary is not a license to reconstruct a fact.

Choose checks at approximation changes, pivotal lemmas, closure assumptions,
limit exchanges, or before downstream reliance. A check should test a concrete
failure mode. Retain explicit project review rules and machine-enforced gates;
progress saving, scientific validation and asking the user are separate actions.
A pending gate can be resolved autonomously only by its authorized evidence path.

For independent review, supply the exact claim, hypotheses, domains, source
artifacts and required obligations. Let the reviewer derive its own checks;
do not require a second full derivation by default or prime it with a desired
verdict. A counterexample, symbolic check or controlled numerical comparison
can be decisive for the relevant obligation; numerical agreement alone is not
proof. Writer-context reasoning cannot stand in for required independent review.

Reasoning effort is a runtime setting. Honor the effective task setting; do not
claim to change it through prose or assume an API feature exists in the runtime client.
An explicitly enforced translation or project model route remains authoritative.
